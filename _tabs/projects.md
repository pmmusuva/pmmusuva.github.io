## Cyber Shujaa Program

**Role:** Co-Founder & Curriculum and Training Director &nbsp;·&nbsp; Mar 2022 – present

A consortium of Serianu Ltd, USIU-Africa, and Kenya Bankers Association delivering hands-on cybersecurity workforce training.

- Trained **5,000+ youth** from **80+ tertiary institutions**, **80%+ job placement**, **41% women's participation**
- Designed and accredited curricula for Cyber Security Analyst, Cloud & Network Security, Data Protection, and Entrepreneurship tracks with the USIU-Africa University Senate
- Funded by a **£200,000 Challenge Fund for Youth Employment (CFYE)** 4-year matched consortium grant
- [Program website](https://cybershujaa.co.ke/)

## CIRI–Serianu Research Partnership

**Role:** Research Partner &nbsp;·&nbsp; 12-year partnership (ongoing)

A long-running research collaboration with Serianu Limited and USIU-Africa producing the annual **Kenya/Africa Cybersecurity Reports**, the most widely cited regional cybersecurity benchmarking series.

- Research grants exceeding **€200,000** over the partnership
- See the [Publications](/publications/) page for the full list of annual reports

## Tatua Digital Resilience Center

**Role:** Consultant, Digital Resilience Strategy &nbsp;·&nbsp; KICTANet / Ford Foundation Weaving Resilience initiative

Developed a digital resilience strategy framework, policy manual, incident-response playbooks, and assessment scorecard for **150+ social justice organizations** in East Africa.

- Trained 100+ board and executive members
- Delivered under the Ford Foundation's Weaving Resilience initiative
