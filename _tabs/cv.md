## Academic & Professional Experience

**Sept 2011 – present — Assistant Professor**, School of Science & Technology, USIU-Africa, Kenya
: Taught 1,500+ students across Information Security, Systems Audit, Cybercrime, Digital Forensics, and Programming (Python, C#). Pioneered university partnerships with Cisco, IBM, and EC-Council. Led development of the MSc in Information Security (MSEC) program and program reviews for the MSc in Information Systems (MIST). Supervised 19+ MSc theses and currently supervises 2 PhD candidates. Built a 12-year Information & Cyber Security research partnership with Serianu Limited, producing the annual Kenya/Africa Cybersecurity Reports and securing research grants exceeding €200,000. Provided expert response support during a ransomware incident.

**Mar 2022 – present — Co-founder & Curriculum and Training Director**, Cyber Shujaa Program, Kenya
: Designed and accredited curricula for Cyber Security Analyst, Cloud & Network Security, Data Protection, and Entrepreneurship tracks with the USIU-Africa University Senate. Leads a team of 6+ trainers delivering training across 9 cohorts. Program has trained 5,000+ youth from 80+ tertiary institutions, 80%+ placed in decent employment, 41% women's participation. [cybershujaa.co.ke](https://cybershujaa.co.ke)

**Jan 2017 – May 2021 — Board Member (Academic Relations); Director, Special Programs**, ISACA Kenya Chapter
: Doubled student membership and expanded strategic partnerships with USIU-Africa, Strathmore, JKUAT, Riara, University of Nairobi. Founded the ISACA inter-varsity bootcamp series, training 180+ students from 17 universities on CISA and CSX Fundamentals. Established the chapter's first One in Tech initiative for gender inclusion in GRC.

**Mar 2015 – Aug 2018 — Team Leader, Implementation, Training & Monitoring**, Institutional Review Board, USIU-Africa
: Developed Standard Operating Procedures enabling IRB establishment and accreditation; trained faculty on research-ethics compliance.

**Feb 2010 – Sept 2010 — Senior Consultant**, Deloitte East Africa, Kenya
: Led IT transformation for Tuskys Supermarket. Guided design of an integrated fraud-detection and policy-management database for the Association of Kenya Insurers (AKI). Contributed to the strategic ICT review of the National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF).

**Nov 2008 – Jan 2010 — Team Leader, Service Management Center**, Seven Seas Technologies, Kenya
: Led a 10+ engineer team delivering ITIL-compliant service-desk operations for clients including Safaricom, KACC, IDRC, and NSSF. Established remote infrastructure monitoring using HP BTO and open-source tools (Nagios, GroundWork, Hyperic HQ).

**Aug 2007 — ITIL Process Administrator**, Dairy Farmers of Britain, United Kingdom
: Documented ITIL-aligned service processes and supported service-desk upgrade and staff training.

**May 2005 – Dec 2006 — Network & Security Engineer**, Seven Seas Technologies, Kenya
: Delivered network and security projects across Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, and Ethiopia, including firewall/VPN deployments and ISO17799/BS7799-based security policy development. Named Employee of the Year.
